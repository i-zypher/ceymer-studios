The one guy.

I'm Corey. I was born in West Virginia, but I've been in Pennsylvania for over 20 years. I love Pittsburgh. It's home.

I taught myself to code on a tablet and kept going until the apps were in the stores. Working with AI is a real strong suit of mine. I believe it's going to change the world for the better, and that we'll learn to coexist.

I love working with people and exploring what interests them. I've traveled through Asia, Europe and the Middle East. My free time goes to writing, whether that's music, books or code. I keep a small group of friends close, I love being outside in nature, and I have a serious caffeine addiction.
