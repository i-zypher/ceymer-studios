Questions

Do my customers need an app?

No. iPhones from the XS onward and nearly all Android phones open your review page with a single tap. For the rare phone without NFC, the Get Found Bundle and our websites include a short review link you can text or print on receipts.

Do I have to give you my Google password?

No. You add us as a manager on your Business Profile, and you can remove us at any time. You always stay the owner.

What if I already have a website?

Start with the Get Found Bundle. If your site needs work we'll tell you honestly what's worth fixing and what isn't.

Can the cards point somewhere else later?

Yes. The cards point to a short link we manage for you, so if your review page or business name changes, the cards keep working.

Who gets the senior rate?

Anyone 65 or older gets 50% off their first hour of tech support or training. After that it's the regular hourly rate.

How do I pay?

Buy anything on this page online with a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through secure Stripe checkout. Websites can be paid in full or with a deposit, with the balance invoiced at launch. In person we also take Cash App for Business ($CeymerStudios) or check.
